Timeline labels

Mutually exclusive per speaker tier · uncertainty is recorded separately in the N+3 flags tier (Tier 6 in the three-speaker Gold reference)

sSpeechLexical speech produced by the current floor holder.Counts as phonation. Laughter embedded in intelligible speech remains s.
fFilled hesitationNonlexical hesitation by the floor holder, including um, uh, er, humming or a prolonged vowel.Counts as phonation and hesitation. The speaker retains the floor.
bcBackchannelA short supportive response from a non-holder.All four tests must pass: no more than three words or purely nonlexical; majority lexical tokens in the versioned backchannel lexicon; no turn-projecting start; holder continues. It does not transfer the floor or create overlap by itself.

opOwn pauseThe floor holder is silent but retains the floor.If the same holder resumes, the entire intervening silence is op, regardless of length. Values above L receive a review flag.
pfPause while floor heldA non-holder is silent while another speaker holds the floor.Represents listening time. It is not an individual hesitation pause.

Floor assignment rules

R1-R4 maintain the floor state; R5 resolves silence retrospectively

R1

Start FREE

The conversational floor is FREE at task start. No speaker is assumed to hold it before the first turn-taking vocalisation.

R2

Claim floor

The first turn-taking vocalisation from a FREE state claims the floor for that speaker.

R3

Retain floor

Silence by the holder and qualifying backchannels from another speaker do not release or transfer the floor.

R4

Competing turn

If a non-holder makes a true turn attempt, the floor transfers when the holder ends only if the attempt survives. If it stops while the holder continues, it is a failed bid with no transfer. Ambiguous multi-speaker competition is flagged.

R5

Resolve silence

Same holder resumes: op. Another speaker enters within L: tr. Another enters after L, or nobody resumes before task end: the whole interval is shs.

When nobody holds the floor

shs is assigned only while the floor is FREE. Parameter G may later support responsibility analysis, but it does not change the timeline label from shs.

Label versus review flag

x is a formal label. Low confidence, ambiguity, boundary conflict and other review reasons remain separate N+3-tier flags so the acoustic event and the quality state are never conflated.
